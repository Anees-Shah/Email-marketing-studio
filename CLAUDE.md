# Email Marketing Studio

This studio is for planning and writing email campaigns for a small business.

## Folders

- `brand/` holds the business's brand details, such as its voice, style and key messages.
- `leads/` holds information about potential customers and who each campaign is for.
- `campaigns/` holds campaign plans and the emails written for them.
- `notes/` holds research, decisions and general notes about the studio.

## Rules

- Write in plain English.
- Never send email to real people. Sending through Resend is allowed only to Resend's test addresses (ending in @resend.dev) and to my own email address. A guard will enforce this in the next step.
- Ask me before deleting anything.
