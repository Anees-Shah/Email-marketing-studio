# Brand voice guide

AI Automation Solutions makes apps that save people time. We sell to organizations such as schools, clinics and restaurants. After reading our emails, people should feel confident that their tasks can be automated.

## Our voice in three words

- **Friendly:** we sound like a helper who is easy to approach.
- **Simple:** we explain things clearly, without technical language.
- **Confident:** we show readers that their tasks can be automated.

## Words we use

- save time
- simple

## Words we avoid

- revolutionary
- AI-powered

## Claims we must never make

- "fully replaces your staff"
- "100% error-free"
- "meets all medical privacy laws"
