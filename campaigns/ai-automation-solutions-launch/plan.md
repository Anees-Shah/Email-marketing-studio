# AI Automation Solutions waitlist launch: campaign plan

**Status:** Draft. Items marked "to confirm" need your input before any email is written.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Simple Start
- **Summary:** A four-week, email-only launch that invites our waitlist to start a subscription to AI Automation Solutions.
- **Main goal:** 300 of about 2,000 waitlist members start a subscription between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Learn which of the three groups responds best.
  - Collect the tasks people most want to automate, to guide future apps and emails.

**How big is this goal?** 300 out of 2,000 is 15% of the list, which is ambitious for email alone. If 25% of people who visit the sign-up page subscribe, 1,200 people (60% of the list) would need to visit it. That 25% is an example, not a forecast. Treat 300 as a stretch goal until week 1 gives us real numbers.

## 2. Target audience

Everyone has already joined the waitlist, so they know us and are close to deciding. The job of this campaign is to help them decide. Exact group sizes are to confirm.

The everyday tasks below are starting ideas. Keep only those our apps really handle.

| Group | Everyday tasks to automate | What they care about | Likely worries |
|---|---|---|---|
| School owners and admins | Attendance, fee reminders, parent messages | Fewer repeated tasks and clear communication with parents | Staff taking time to learn it; safe handling of student information |
| Restaurant owners and admins | Bookings, staff schedules, stock orders | Time back during busy shifts and a quick setup | Cost; disruption during service |
| Clinic admins | Appointment reminders, intake forms, follow-up messages | Fewer missed appointments and less desk work | Privacy of patient information; reliability |

**Audience profile:** An owner or admin at a small organization who is short on time because of repeated tasks and wants a simple way to automate them. They care most about whether it will work for them, how easy it is to start and what it costs.

## 3. Key messages

**Core message:** Your repeated tasks can be automated, and getting started is simple.

| Reader's question | Supporting message | Proof needed (to confirm) |
|---|---|---|
| Why should I care? | Repeated tasks use up time your team could spend elsewhere. | A real example of a task and the steps it removes |
| What is it? | Our apps take care of the everyday tasks you choose. | A short demo or screenshots of a working app |
| Why you? | We help you start with one task and stay on hand if you need us. | Actual setup steps and support details; customer quotes if we have permission |
| What should I do? | Tell us your biggest time-consuming task, or start your subscription today. | Sign-up page and offer details |

**Change by group:** Keep the core message the same. Change the opening line and the example task for each group.

**Voice:** Follow `brand/voice.md`. Be friendly, simple and confident. In particular:

- Use "save time" and "simple" where they fit naturally.
- Do not use "revolutionary" or "AI-powered".
- Do not say or imply that we fully replace staff, are 100% error-free or meet all medical privacy laws.
- Do not quote a time saving unless we have real evidence for it.

## 7. Success metrics

**Main measure:** subscriptions started, with a target of 300.

| Checkpoint | Total subscriptions |

| End of week 2 (Sun 20 Dec) | 150 |
| End of week 3 (Sun 27 Dec) | 200 |

These checkpoints are planning targets, not forecasts. They assume strong interest at launch and a final push near the deadline.

| Secondary measure | How we track it |
|---|---|
| Emails delivered and bounced | Sending tool |
| Click rate for each email and group | Sending tool, with a different link for each group |
| Sign-up page visitors who subscribe | Page analytics and sign-up records |
| Unsubscribes and spam complaints | Sending tool |

Treat open rates as a rough signal only, because some email apps make them unreliable.

**Reporting:** Check the numbers every Monday, and write a final report on 4 January in `campaigns/ai-automation-solutions-launch/`.

## 9. Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do about it |
|---|---|
| **The holiday period.** In many places, late December is a slow time: schools may be closed, restaurants may be very busy and clinics may be short of staff. | Check local holidays for each group. Get the strongest emails out by 17 December, and be ready to move or extend the later ones. |
| **We fall short of 300.** 15% is a high target for email alone. | Review after week 1. If we are under 60, improve the offer, focus on the most responsive group and have Anees follow up personally with people who clicked or replied. |
| **The list has gone cold or has bad addresses.** | Remove bad addresses before launch. Start with a smaller send if the list is old, and watch bounces and complaints. |
| **Privacy or overpromising, especially with clinics and schools.** | Use only claims we can back up. Have clear answers about data handling, get qualified advice where needed and never ask for patient or student details by email. |
| **Email rules.** | Confirm everyone agreed to hear from us. Include our contact details and an unsubscribe link in every email, and check the rules in each country we email. |
