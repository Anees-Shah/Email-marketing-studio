# AI Automation Solutions waitlist launch: campaign plan

**Status:** Draft, updated with Anees's final answers. Three new questions are listed in section 10.

## 1. Campaign overview

- **Campaign name:** Simple Start
- **Summary:** A four-week, email-only launch that shows our waitlist the app made for their kind of organization and invites them to order it.
- **Main goal:** 300 of about 2,000 waitlist members become new customers between Monday 7 December 2026 and Sunday 3 January 2027.
- **What counts as a new customer:** An organization that buys any app or starts the clinic booking system, counted once its bank transfer is confirmed. An organization buying two apps counts once.
- **Secondary goals:**
  - Learn which group and app get the strongest response.
  - Collect the tasks people most want to automate, to guide future apps and emails.

**How big is this goal?** 300 out of 2,000 is 15% of the list, which is ambitious for email alone, and these are paid business purchases. If 25% of people who visit the order page buy, 1,200 people (60% of the list) would need to visit it. That 25% is an example, not a forecast. Treat 300 as a stretch goal until week 1 gives us real numbers.

## 2. What we are selling

School and restaurant prices are in rupees. Clinic prices are in dollars because that app is intended for an international market.

| App | For | Price |
|---|---|---|
| School Certificate Generator | Schools | PKR 12,000 one-time |
| School Marks Sheet / Transcript Generator | Schools | PKR 6,000 one-time |
| Restaurant Order Booking App | Restaurants | PKR 20,000 one-time |
| Clinic Online Patient Booking System | Clinics | $80 initial cost + $50 per month |

We have a demo video for every app. There is no waitlist offer, so no email should mention a discount, a deadline or limited availability.

**What the price includes:** Installation only. Emails and the order page must not promise ongoing support or updates.

**How buying works:**

1. The customer places an order on the order page.
2. The customer receives bank transfer details and pays by bank transfer.
3. We confirm the payment.
4. Our team contacts the customer and guides them through installation.

## 3. Target audience

Everyone has already joined the waitlist, so they know us and are close to deciding. The job of this campaign is to help them decide.

Planning targets split the 300 evenly at 15% of each group. Group sizes are approximate, and we still need to check that the list labels each person by group.

| Group | Size | Target | App to show | What they care about | Likely worries |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| School owners and admins | About 800 | 120 | Certificate Generator and Marks Sheet / Transcript Generator | Less repeated paperwork and correct documents | Whether it fits their format; handling student information safely |
| Restaurant owners and admins | About 700 | 105 | Order Booking App | Taking orders simply, even during busy shifts | Cost; disruption during service |
| Clinic admins | About 500 | 75 | Online Patient Booking System | Simple booking for patients and less desk work | Patient privacy; reliability; the monthly cost |

**Audience profile:** An owner or admin at a small organization who is short on time because of repeated tasks and wants a simple way to automate them. They care most about whether it will work for them, how easy it is to start and what it costs.

## 4. Key messages

**Core message:** Your repeated tasks can be automated, and getting started is simple.

| Reader's question | Supporting message | Proof |
|---|---|---|
| Why should I care? | Repeated tasks use up time your team could spend elsewhere. | A real example of the task for this group |
| What is it? | Here is the app made for your kind of organization. | The demo video for that app |
| Why you? | You can watch the app working before you decide, and the price is clear. | Demo video and the price table in section 2 |
| What should I do? | Watch the demo, then order, or reply with your question. | Order page |

We have no customer quotes or results to use yet, so emails must not mention any.

**Change by group:** Keep the core message the same. Change the opening line, the example, the demo video and the price for each group. Schools see both school apps.

**Voice:** Follow `brand/voice.md`. Be friendly, simple and confident. In particular:

- Use "save time" and "simple" where they fit naturally.
- Do not use "revolutionary" or "AI-powered".
- Do not say or imply that we fully replace staff, are 100% error-free or meet all medical privacy laws.
- Do not quote a time saving unless we have real evidence for it.

## 9. Budget and risks

### Budget

The budget is small and exact figures are to confirm. Most of the cost will be your time.

| Item | Type | Plan |
|---|---|---|
| Resend | Distribution | Choose a plan that covers about 2,000 contacts and up to 14,000 emails (seven emails to everyone) |
| Order page | Production | Use an existing or low-cost form, as bank transfers need no payment tool |
| Writing | Production | Done in this studio, with simple text-focused emails |
| Demo videos | Production | Already made |
| Paid ads | Distribution | None |
| Contingency | Reserve | Keep 10 to 15% for a tool upgrade or a surprise cost |

### Risks and how we handle them

| Risk | What we do about it |
|---|---|
| **The holiday period.** Anees chose to keep the 7 December start, but schools may be on winter break and restaurants may be busy late in the month. | Check dates for each group. Get the main emails out by 17 December, and be ready to move later school emails. |
| **We fall short of 300.** 15% is a high target for email alone, and schools and clinics may need approval before buying. | Review after week 1. If we are under 60, improve the demo and message, focus on the most responsive group and have Anees follow up personally with people who clicked or replied. |
| **People order but do not finish the bank transfer.** Paying this way takes extra steps and is checked by hand. | Send bank details quickly, use a payment reference for each order, follow up on unpaid orders and track orders and payments separately. |
| **Too many installations at once.** Reaching 300 customers means up to 300 guided installations in about a month. | Agree how many our team can handle each week, and tell customers when to expect contact. Slow the emails if orders get ahead of installations. |
| **Customers expect support after installation.** The price includes installation only. | Say this clearly on the order page and in the questions and answers, and have the team repeat it during installation. |
| **International clinics find monthly bank transfers awkward.** Transfers abroad can be slow and may carry fees, and the $50 charge repeats every month. | Confirm how this will work before launch, and explain it clearly on the order page. Show each group only its own price and currency. |
| **The list has gone cold or has bad addresses.** | Remove bad addresses before launch. Start with a smaller send if the list is old, and watch bounces and complaints. |
| **Privacy or overpromising, especially with clinics and schools.** | Use only claims we can back up. Have clear answers about data handling, get qualified advice where needed and never ask for patient or student details by email. |
| **Email rules.** | Confirm everyone agreed to hear from us. Include our contact details and an unsubscribe link in every email, and check the rules in each country we email. |
